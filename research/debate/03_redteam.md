# Red Team note: K4, K5, H, C

Verification done on arXiv abstract/HTML pages on 22 Oct 2026; ids inline.

## K4 Replica-Consensus Loop

**Guarantee.** (i) is Banach, but F(H)=H+B(H,E) has Lipschitz constant up to 1+Lip(B): spectral-normalising B cannot certify λ<1; a residual map contracts only if J_B ≈ −I. 2609.26487 shows converged recursive states are contractive along the trajectory with strongly expanding Jacobian directions, so no convex set containing the trajectories is a contraction domain; λ must be measured, and (ii) then says only "not a contraction for the λ you assumed". (iii) "False alarms impossible" is false: slow (λ≈1) or OOD-length inputs give D^{Kmax}>ε with one attractor, and the consensus penalty acts only on training inputs, so h=16 chains (trained h≤8) are exactly where F is uncertified. The deeper flaw: D^k measures decay of the injected noise, not completion. Replicas share weights and E(X); they differ only by the residual of σξ, which decays at rate λ independent of hop count. A λ=0.5 model reaches D<ε by k≈8 on a 16-hop chain and halts wrong; λ=0.95 never halts. Fix: halt on ‖H^k−H^{k−1}‖≤ε (Huginn's test); keep D only as the ambiguity flag. Second flaw: at a two-edge fork softmax attention returns the mean of both successors (A2: mean only), a unique fixed point; both replicas converge to the blend and D→0. The p^R bound assumes bistability that attention+MLP does not supply; it needs a competitive nonlinearity (K2's energy MLP).

**C-class.** C1: no gate, but σ and ε jointly set halting time, so halting is tuned, not learned. C2: deep supervision plus consensus penalty reward contraction to one point on every training input; nothing rewards bistability; ambiguity is test-only, so the interrupt is untrained by construction. C3: no scale evidence; R=2 halves the iteration budget while 2609.26487 shows Sudoku exact-solve still rising from 16 to 512 steps, so the equal-compute loss is real. C4: R× loop FLOPs and memory; parallel-friendly.

**Prior art.** 2211.09961 confirmed but understated: Anil et al. measure path independence per example from different initialisations and report it correlates with per-example accuracy; that is K4's statistic. 2502.05171 confirmed (s_0~N(0,σ²I); KL exit <5·10⁻⁴). 2605.21488 confirmed (B restarts, majority vote, smallest-residual Top-1, learned q̂_k=f_φ(z_k)); it also measures path independence across restarts. 2601.19085 misattributed: halt-first is across an ensemble of TRM models; the K=4 latent states are trained by lowest-loss winner, not first-to-halt. 2609.26487 confirmed, and it cuts against (i). 2607.11116 is a port-Hamiltonian DEQ study, not looped transformers; its no-op diagnostic transfers. Closer: PABEE 2006.04152 (halt when consecutive exits agree) and self-consistency 2203.11171 (agreement as confidence).

**Kill test.** The forked/unforked ratio and the KL-exit kill are well designed. "Halt by h+2" conflates contraction speed with hop count: a pass needs ε set so λ^kσ crosses ε near k=h, which is tuning. Tighter: report halting-step-vs-h slope (should be 1); add the convergence-halt control; use R=4 (miss 1/8 instead of 1/2, otherwise the forked median sits on the miss boundary); add three-successor forks to separate blend from bistable.

**Verdict:** dead as a halting rule; survives with the fix as an ambiguity flag on a block with multiple attractors (K2). P(beats K0)=0.12: it costs R× and buys abstention.

## K5 Backtracking workspace

**Guarantee.** The DFS induction holds with hard actions, but pop stores one mark u(w'); a node with b children needs up to b−1 exclusions per level, so marks accumulate additively in w, superposition again, s(b−1) marks in (s+1)d. The separation is real (polynomial vs b^s) but "state independent of frontier" holds only up to C(d) marks per level, and "T never re-proposes an excluded sibling" is learned, not guaranteed. With soft actions (Joulin–Mikolov) the stack is a convex blend of push/pop/stay histories; DFS semantics exist only in the hard limit, so the 2N bound is a test-time property of a model trained on a different object (exposure gap). DAgger relabelling needs the solver to read w, i.e. an unstated decoder from latent to node. Learnability obstacle: at s=5 the tick must propose children of groups never seen in training, which is positional generalisation in the fast stream, a known failure; use relative or no positional encoding and content group tokens.

**C-class.** C1: a 3-way discrete action, but trace-supervised, so collapse risk is below REVISE's; the free part is u(w'). C2: only traced or RL data reward a pop; on prose, dead weight. C3: none; token-space competitors already scale (SoS 2404.03683; Self-Backtracking 2502.04404, +30-40% on Countdown at 1-3B). C4: O(d²+n_e d) per tick, (s+1)d memory, cheap; but worst-case b^s ticks serialise inference and training unrolls through every tick.

**Prior art.** 2507.15343 confirmed (hidden-state stacks between layers, soft push/pop, 360M-7B; Dyck not in abstract). 2310.01749 confirmed. 2602.08100 confirmed (32%; "34% accuracy gain", unit unstated). 2605.22221 partly misattributed: the SSA mask fixes history entanglement; scattered retrieval is fixed by trace localisation. 2603.20219 confirmed. Closer: NPI 1511.06279 and recursive NPI 1704.06611, a call stack trained from execution traces with provable length generalisation once state is localised by recursion: K5's recipe and its generalisation condition, ten years earlier. NS-RNN 2010.04674: nondeterministic (superposed) stacks beat deterministic differentiable stacks on CFLs; the stack-vs-superposition dichotomy is false.

**Kill test.** One-per-group sum has a DP over achievable partial sums (≤46 states at s=5), so a looped K0 carrying a 46-bit achievable-sum set in d=64 can reach ~100% with no DFS; the "K0<50%" criterion can then kill K5 for the wrong reason. Tighter: a CSP with no small DP state (distinct chosen digits plus sum, or 3-colouring) with b^s≥1000≫C(64); add the real competitor, a token-trace backtracker at equal FLOPs; keep pop precision; add train s≤3, test s=8.

**Verdict:** survives with fixes. P(beats K0)=0.20: it wins only where the frontier exceeds superposition, and there it must also beat token-space backtracking, which has no C1 problem.

## H HINDSIGHT

**Guarantee.** The InfoNCE bound and the DPI step are correct; sufficiency is for z_t=Qb_t, as conceded. Two gaps. (1) z_t is co-trained: the joint minimiser over (f,Q,B) makes z_t whatever is easiest to predict from the prefix, and b_t=B(x_{>t}) contains x_{t+1}, so the optimum is the next token plus slow topic features: the NCE term collapses toward next-token prediction, the T3 shortcut itself. 1811.06407 found exactly this: one-step CPC did not yield belief states; multi-step targets were "critical". Fix: contrast s_t with B(x_{>t+k}), k≥8, or a frozen far-suffix bag of tokens. (2) B is shared between the hindsight head and the NCE target; the NCE gradient pushes b toward prefix-predictable content, raising CE_b toward CE_f and shrinking g_t, so the weights decay to uniform during training. Fix: stop-gradient from NCE into B. The stop-gradient on g_t does not break the optimum (w_t>0 preserves per-position minimisers). The bilinear critic is not tight, so I(s;z) is lower-bounded only; the 5.5-nat ceiling at batch 256 is correctly conceded.

**C-class.** C1: no gates, no discrete actions. C2: the only candidate whose signal is the project's one positive result (reweighting, 18-35%); on prose the large-g tokens are first mentions of names and numbers, memorisation targets not decisions, so expect ≤1% on FineWeb, consistent with 2410.23771 and 2503.09202. C3: reweighting gains persisted at 1-7B (2404.07965, 2410.23771), the best scale position of the four; but it adds no depth, so T1 tasks are untouched. C4: two backward layers over the suffix (~+30-50% training FLOPs at small L), batch NCE O(BTd), zero inference cost, no serialisation.

**Prior art.** 2410.23506 confirmed; Theorem 2 is for the ideal model, and its backward encoder is already effectively discarded at inference (precomputed B(∅)), so that is not new. 1807.03748, 1905.06922, 1811.04251 confirmed (O(ln N)). 2410.23771, 2503.09202 confirmed as long-short prefix-gap weighting. 2404.07965, 2506.01939, 2411.19943, 2504.11336 confirmed at abstract level. Closer: 1811.06407 (CPC belief states with the sufficiency framing and multi-step necessity); 2503.10542 (path-star solved by supervision changes alone, a cheaper baseline).

**Kill test.** The harness arms and precision-0.5 criterion are right. Path-star is not decisive: 2403.06963 Prop. 3 says learning from the first token alone can take exponential time, so weight-only cannot pass; a pass would come from the NCE term acting as teacherless/multi-token prediction (2403.06963; 2404.19737), not from "the gap finds decisions". Tighter: four arms (weight-only, NCE-only, both, hand labels), and report g_t's precision against hand labels before α ramps.

**Verdict:** survives with the two fixes. P(K0+H beats K0 at equal compute on a long-horizon decision benchmark)=0.35; on depth-bound puzzles 0.05. The only candidate that attacks T3 directly, costs nothing at inference, and has 1-7B precedents.

## C CLOSURE

**Guarantee.** Kleene is correct for the hashed relation, which is the quotient graph, whose reachability is a superset of the truth, and the error compounds across hops: one collision between a reachable and an unreachable node imports the latter's whole descendant set. The Bloom formula bounds membership, not closure; and with κ hashes in one bit array the propagation mixes hash functions, so closure semantics survive only with κ separate (E_i,F_i) and an AND readout. At the builder's own n=64, S=4096: P(any collision)=1−e^{−n²/2S}≈0.39 per sequence; at the extrapolation target n=256, 8 expected colliding pairs, closure false positives of order 1 per hash, and after AND over κ=2 a few percent of unreachable nodes flagged: the 90% criterion fails from hashing alone. Fix: addresses = context positions (exact, no hash, no T2 term), which is 2402.01107; or S≈2²⁰ with sparse E.

**C-class.** C1: two STE-trained discrete maps (sign(Wu), thresholded relation head); the project's hashing win used a fixed hash, and its gates collapsed. C2: with frontier supervision it is a programmed BFS; without it, gradient through K boolean ORs via STE is ~0, so on iGSM-style text the relation head is unlearnable. C3: 2405.09220 shows transformers store adjacency and limited reachability in weights, and CoT computes closures in tokens; the niche shrinks with size. C4: 2 MB and 16.8M bit-ops per step per sequence, fine; ingest is n² pair tests; dense E is impossible at collision-free S. B4 is lost (monotone), contradicting the project's "no, wait" goal.

**Prior art.** 1906.04304 confirmed (one-shot approximate membership by meta-learning). 2505.12514 confirmed (D-step BFS by superposition); C(d)=Θ(d/log n) is the builder's gloss. 2509.23365 misattributed: the paper says the index-matching logit stays bounded so superposition emerges generically, the opposite of "only in a narrow regime". 2606.18206 confirmed. 2603.15641 partly: convergence is a reliability signal, not a halting rule. Closer: 2402.01107 (looped transformer with graph-structured heads runs BFS/DFS/Dijkstra exactly with size-independent parameters: CLOSURE without hashing); 2405.09220; BMLP 2408.10369 (boolean-matrix Datalog is the Kleene iteration); ∂ILP 1711.04574.

**Kill test.** Not decisive either way. With n=64=d, one-hot node embeddings make the frontier an exact 64-bit vector, so the K0 control can reach 95% at F=32 and C "dies" though capacity was never tested; and C's pass is guaranteed by frontier supervision on a task where the relation is a literal bigram. Tighter: n≥1024≫d, no frontier supervision, relations stated indirectly (two-hop rules), compare against 2402.01107's graph-head looped transformer at equal FLOPs.

**Verdict:** dead as proposed; survives only as positions-as-addresses, which is K0 with an exact relation head. P(beats K0)=0.10.

## Ranking and combination

H > K5 > K4 > C.

Strongest pair: **K0 + H.** They attack the two barriers that do not shrink with scale and are orthogonal: K0 buys sequential depth (T1) and H buys an objective that forces the state to carry the future at the positions where the future matters (T3). Neither has a free gate, a router, or a discrete action, which is every collapse this project has recorded; both have precedents above 1B (2502.05171, 2510.25741; 2404.07965, 2410.23771); H adds zero inference cost, so the equal-compute comparison is clean. The test is already specified: dilution harness with the four-arm ablation, then V1 with K0+H as a fourth arm against K0. If the pair works, g_t also supplies for free what K5 lacks, a label for commitment positions, and what V1 wants, the position where late evidence lands: it is the on-ramp to B4 rather than a competitor to it. K4's D, with the convergence fix, is a cheap diagnostic to log alongside, not a mechanism to build on.
