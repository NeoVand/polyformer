**1. “Specific indirect experience teaches a grammatical rule never demonstrated.” — PARTIAL**

This is language-acquisition research: controlled changes to learning experience distinguish explanations of grammatical knowledge.

**Most dangerous:** [Leong & Linzen, 2026, *Journal of Memory and Language* 149:104751](https://tallinzen.net/media/papers/leong_linzen_2026_jml.pdf) already combined human judgments with corpus removal, alteration and insertion to identify frequency and semantic contributions to passive restrictions.

[Yao, Misra, Weissweiler & Mahowald, 2025, arXiv:2503.20850](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20850) manipulated direct and indirect evidence for dative preferences. [Yang, Bisazza, Schneider & Wilcox, 2026, arXiv:2602.09992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09992) tested four grammatical generalizations under limited input, including 10-million-word training.

**Remaining contribution:** discover a compact teaching set, then prospectively demonstrate its predicted transfer in randomized human learning. Removing/restoring training examples alone is OWNED.

**Decision:** strongest balance of tractability and explanatory value. Assume an adult miniature-language result is acceptable; it cannot establish childhood acquisition. Existing architectures defer condition 10 but isolate learning experience. Kill if matched unrelated examples teach equally well or human transfer fails.

**2. “A machine discovers a previously unknown human grammatical restriction.” — PARTIAL; the narrow discovery remains OPEN**

Grammar discovery means inferring rules from examples or judgments; its value here is a new prediction about speakers.

**Most dangerous for novelty:** [Yu, Yu, Xiao & Xing, 2026, arXiv:2604.07189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07189) already automated hypothesis generation, corpus investigation and linguistic findings, including replication of published studies.

[Carrasco, Mayr & Yovine, 2024, arXiv:2412.09760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09760) developed algebraic equivalence classes and an algorithm for learning regular probabilistic language models. [Hu, Wilcox, Song, Mahowald & Levy, 2026, *TACL* 14:124–146](https://aclanthology.org/2026.tacl-1.7/) showed why meaning-controlled sentence contrasts support grammatical evaluation better than unpaired probabilities.

**Remaining contribution:** an unfamiliar restriction, independently checked against linguistic literature, confirmed on new human judgments, and explained by a compact extracted rule. Model agreement or category clustering is insufficient.

**Decision:** highest upside, lowest predictable yield. Assume a discoverable restriction exists. External extraction relaxes condition 2 and buys scientific interpretability. Treat finite-context classes as approximations: they do not establish a finite exact algebra for unrestricted English. Kill if every confirmed candidate is already known.

**3. “Some grammars disappear because speakers cannot reliably transmit them.” — PARTIAL**

Cultural transmission means learners acquiring language from previous learners; it separates initial learnability from survival across generations.

**Most dangerous:** [Janarthan, Haley & Goldwater, 2026, *CoNLL*, 2026.conll-main.24](https://aclanthology.org/2026.conll-main.24/) already found relatively preserved grammatical sensitivity but poor long-sentence production, explicitly proposing transmission failure.

[Guo, Wu & Yiu, 2026, arXiv:2605.23054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23054) studied ten self-training generations and linguistic degradation through iterated-learning theory. [Galke, Ram & Raviv, 2024, *Nature Communications* 15:10816](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55158-1) showed that humans and networks both learn and generalize more successfully from compositional languages.

**Remaining contribution:** equalize initial mastery, manipulate production-memory support, and predict a reversal in actual human transmission chains.

**Decision:** reject A4’s “settles impossible languages” headline. Equal automaton size does not equal psychological difficulty; [Hunter, 2025, *Computational Linguistics* 51:641–650](https://aclanthology.org/2025.cl-2.7/) identifies a confounded comparison in that debate. Assume timely recruitment and approval; this relaxes condition 12’s budget. Kill if transmission losses merely reproduce initial learning differences.

**4. “Three-item interactions predict human relational learning.” — PARTIAL**

Three-item interactions depend jointly on three objects; they provide a testable explanation for the SET advantage.

**Most dangerous:** [Shang, Kreiman & Sompolinsky, 2026, *Scientific Reports* 16:22146](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13369344/) already compared relational architectures with humans and connected representation geometry to generalization and difficulty.

[Altabaa, Webb, Cohen & Lafferty, 2024, *ICLR*](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/6b070264f20d25fbd1488de8ea51575f-Abstract-Conference.html) introduced Abstractors, separating relational information from object features. [Sanford, Hsu & Telgarsky, 2023, *NeurIPS*, arXiv:2306.02896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02896) established attention limitations on triple detection and higher-order constructions. **A3’s arXiv identifier is wrong.**

**Remaining contribution:** prospectively predict human errors and learning curves on tasks with matched pairwise statistics but differing triple structure, outperforming recurrent and relational alternatives.

**Decision:** best inexpensive bridge from existing assets to cognitive science. Assume interaction order predicts behaviour beyond task difficulty. Preserve architectural ambition, but postpone certification. Kill if ordinary depth or recurrence explains the same results. The audit supplies no universal impossibility theorem against those controls.

**5. “Learning entity identity before transitions repairs fragile world models.” — PARTIAL, heavily crowded**

State tracking means maintaining what is currently true after successive events; aliases test whether models track entities rather than repeated names.

**Most dangerous:** [Sarkar, Mukherjee & Sato, 2026, arXiv:2610.34945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34945) already introduced per-entity relational operators, representation-dimension results, certificates and compositional reasoning experiments.

[Vafa, Chen, Rambachan, Kleinberg & Mullainathan, 2024, *NeurIPS*, arXiv:2406.03689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03689) exposed incoherent world models using distinguishing continuations. [Grazzi, Siems, Zela, Franke, Hutter & Pontil, 2025, *ICLR*, arXiv:2411.12537](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12537) expanded recurrent state-tracking capabilities through negative eigenvalues.

**Remaining contribution:** learned identity resolution plus deliberately distinguishing future questions that transfers across aliases, irrelevant text and detours without supplied entity parsing.

**Decision:** strongest startup fit, weaker Nature case. Assume the audit’s defective supervision can be repaired; failure was not proof that averaging futures is inherently wrong. Compare against the same training objective on conventional recurrence. Supplying parsed entities would relax condition 3 and substantially weaken the claim.

**6. “A representative sample of mathematics contains at least this many certified false claims.” — PARTIAL**

This is mathematical metascience: measuring reliability using independently checkable counterexamples.

**Most dangerous:** [She & Wang, 2026, arXiv:2610.35596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35596) introduced KAPPA, checking papers with Lean and recovering published errors plus additional manually confirmed problems.

[Bianchi, Kwon, Izzo, Zhang & Zou, 2025, arXiv:2512.05925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05925) quantified errors in published AI papers with human validation. [Bastounis, Circelli & Hansen, 2026, arXiv:2610.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08144) demonstrated that verified formal translations can misrepresent original mathematical claims.

**Remaining contribution:** a prespecified population, representative sampling, faithful statement checking and certified counterexamples. Count false statements separately from defective proofs and typos.

**Decision:** retain as a wildcard, not the main programme. Assume enough substantive errors are discoverable; independent statement checking strains solo feasibility. Preserves condition 5, postpones the new-model goal. Failed formalization must never count as falsity.

**Why memory and sentence repair fell out.** [Gong, 2026, arXiv:2610.21176](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21176) already separated retrieval from interference and improved working-memory performance by suppressing distractors. [Ding, 2026, arXiv:2610.36797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36797) causally separated discarded sentence interpretations from adaptation across 24 models. Their broad framings are substantially OWNED.
