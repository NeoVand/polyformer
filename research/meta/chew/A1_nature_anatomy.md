Two corrections change the practical lesson. Lake–Baroni was two-person work; Centaur was **not** solo research: affordable training rested on extensive collective data assembly. Also, **Thorp (2026, Science)** issued an [expression of concern](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aej2383) concerning Costello and colleagues; their subsequent [correction materials](https://zenodo.org/records/22754984) warrant checking before treating the original estimates as settled.

**Recurring recipes for 4–10 weeks**

- **Old question, controlled miniature:** Lake–Baroni and Vong make a large theoretical dispute experimentally tractable. Match competing explanations on data and resources; test where their predictions differ.
- **Unexpected intervention, general consequence:** Betley and Cloud change something narrow and find something broad. Replication across models, matched controls and mechanism distinguish discovery from an anecdote.
- **AI as scientific instrument:** Centaur and Ashokkumar validate predictions against independently collected observations. The valuable asset is often the evaluation dataset.
- **Search plus independent checking:** FunSearch demonstrates discovery by producing an object whose value survives without trusting the model.

A workable schedule: weeks 1–2 establish prior art and stopping criteria; weeks 3–5 run the decisive experiment; weeks 6–8 attack alternative explanations; weeks 9–10 replicate and write. This assumes existing datasets or timely participant access. It estimates a research cycle, not publication time.

**Four concrete directions**

All titles below are hypotheses, not established findings. Novelty remains **unverified**. These alternatives prioritize scientific publication over immediate fulfillment of internal program execution and certification—conditions 3, 5 and 7.

1. **“Language models reveal an unrecognized constraint on human grammar.”**  
   **8–10 weeks; linguistic discovery.** Extract rules from controlled sentence judgments, then freeze one genuinely unfamiliar prediction. **Decisive experiment:** independent speakers confirm it on unseen words and contexts, while competing linguistic explanations predict differently. Closest art: [Goertzel, Suárez-Madrigal & Yu, 2020, arXiv:2005.12533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12533), which guided symbolic grammar learning using transformer probabilities; [Hu, Wilcox, Song, Mahowald & Levy, 2026, TACL](https://aclanthology.org/2026.tacl-1.7/), which establishes why meaning-controlled comparisons matter. Assumption: a discoverable restriction exists. External extraction relaxes condition 2; it buys independently testable linguistic knowledge.

2. **“Learning experience determines neural preferences for human grammars.”**  
   **6–8 weeks; language acquisition.** **Decisive experiment:** cross architecture with training experience under equal data budgets, then predict human learning on withheld miniature languages. Closest art: Lake–Baroni above and [Kallini et al., 2024, ACL](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.787/), which found GPT-2 learned manipulated “impossible” languages less efficiently. Assumption: controlled exposure separates competing explanations. This defers condition 10, a new architecture, to identify which experience actually matters.

3. **“A small set of counterexamples preserves grammar during self-training.”**  
   **4–6 weeks; learning from generated data.** **Decisive experiment:** compare examples chosen to distinguish competing rules against equal-sized random and rarity-based samples over repeated training generations. Require preservation of unseen grammatical distinctions across two model families. Closest art: Shumailov above; [Guo, Wu & Yiu, 2026, arXiv:2605.23054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23054), which already studies linguistic change during self-training. Assumption: distinguishing rules adds value beyond preserving rare examples. Deferring condition 10 buys a cheap, sharply falsifiable intervention.

4. **“Three-way interactions explain human relational generalization.”**  
   **6–10 weeks; cognitive modelling.** Three-way interactions represent relationships that depend jointly on three items; this directly tests your SET advantage. **Decisive experiment:** humans and matched networks learn tasks with identical pairwise statistics but different triple relationships; predict withheld human errors and learning curves. Closest art: [Clift, Doryn, Murfet & Wallbridge, 2020, ICLR](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=rkecJ6VFvr), which introduced three-way attention for logical reasoning, and Webb above. Assumption: the architecture predicts human behaviour beyond accuracy. This preserves condition 10 and converts an existing engineering result into a scientific test.
