**1. Sentence memory — referee verdict: reject the current broad claim; invite a sharply discriminating study.**

This belongs to computational psycholinguistics: explaining reading behaviour through explicit processing models. Standard alternatives already include selective word retention, structural retrieval and noisy representations.

- [Hahn, Futrell, Levy & Gibson (2022, PNAS 119:e2122602119)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9618130/) explained recursive-sentence difficulty through optimized memory and tested distinctive predictions.
- [Yoshida, Isono, Kajikawa, Someya, Sugimoto & Oseki (2025, ACL:9795–9812)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.483.pdf) found structural and token representations independently predicted reading times.
- [Xu, Dillon & Futrell (2026, ACL:33603–33618)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1550.pdf) constrained encoding precision, improving reading-time prediction and producing more categorical representations.
- [Kajikawa, Isono & Wilcox (2026, arXiv:2602.18217)](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18217) predicted reading costs from information past words retain about future context.

Consequently, “memory preserves structure” and “compression creates categories” are occupied claims. Nerode equivalence groups histories that permit identical future answers; it offers exact controls within specified finite grammars. It does not itself predict human forgetting or processing time.

**Fix:** identify one distinction whose loss predicts an error or reading-time contrast that those competitors predict differently. Compare equal storage capacities, equal grammatical information and equal training exposure. Freeze predictions before examining reserved constructions; replicate across independent archives. Erasure/restoration establishes causality inside the model only.

[Nusbaumer, de-Dios-Flores, Bel, Pallier, Wisniewski & Crabbé (2026, Findings EMNLP:28874–28888)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1360.pdf) released StructuralCost and documented underestimated integration costs. Its deeper-nesting speedup is small, and item-level comprehension accuracy is missing; it cannot anchor a comprehension-rescue headline. Download/licence remain **unverified** here.

**2. Reward-learning memories — referee verdict: reject “prediction identifies mechanism”; require a consequential boundary on interpretation.**

This is computational decision science. Standard competitors include reward averaging, remembered events, repeated-choice habits and networks carrying state between trials.

- [Collins (2026 issue; online 2025, Nature Human Behaviour 10:357–369)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12932107/) reanalysed seven datasets, favoured memory-plus-habit explanations, and already checked recovery of models and parameters.
- [Eckstein, Summerfield, Daw & Miller (2026, Nature Human Behaviour 10:972–987)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13212159/) found advantages from flexible memory and tested whether simulated behaviours distinguished competing models.
- [Ji-An, Benna & Mattar (2025, Nature 644:993–1001)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12390849/) extracted interpretable strategies from tiny networks across six existing tasks.
- [Bera, Fengler, Boudewyn, Carter, Erickson, Gold, Luck, Ragland, Yonelinas, MacDonald, Barch & Frank (2026, PLOS Computational Biology 22:e1014796)](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1014796&type=printable) used response times and choices to separate processes and improve held-out predictions.

The omitted last paper further narrows the opening. Routine recovery checks or adding response times will not suffice.

**Fix:** demonstrate that a particular substantive interpretation survives—or fails—across different tasks despite equally predictive alternatives. Locate histories where predictions diverge, then assess their actual frequency and uncertainty in untouched participants. Distinguish exact equivalence from insufficient observations. Discretizing a continuous model introduces an approximation that must be measured.

Assumption: archives contain separating evidence. [Collins’s code/data repository](https://github.com/AnneCollins/WMH) is accessible. **Budget:** $1,000. **Kill within one week** if informative histories are absent or the conclusion merely repeats known ambiguity. Specialist target: Journal of Mathematical Psychology.

**3. Archived relational learning — referee verdict: reject the universal-capacity story; conditionally consider a predictive account of representation change.**

Arity counts the inputs combined in a relation; it matters only relative to a representation and processing strategy.

- [Halford, Wilson & Phillips (1998, Behavioral and Brain Sciences 21:803–831)](https://staff.aist.go.jp/steven.phillips/papers/relational_complexity.pdf) already explained relational limits through joint processing, chunking and sequential decomposition.
- [Feldman (2006, Journal of Mathematical Psychology 50:339–368)](https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/an-algebra-of-human-concept-learning/) decomposed concepts by feature degree; abstract verified, full text **unverified** despite retrieval attempts.
- [Altabaa, Webb, Cohen & Lafferty (2024, ICLR; arXiv:2304.00195)](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.00195) improved relational learning with separated relational representations, including SET.
- [Badham, Sanborn & Maylor (2017, Psychology and Aging 32:473–488)](https://irep.ntu.ac.uk/30691/1/8441_Badham.pdf) found age deficits inconsistent with simple rule-complexity accounts and supported clustering.
- [Shang, Kreiman & Sompolinsky (2026, Scientific Reports 16:22146)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13369344/) linked representation geometry to generalization and human difficulty.

**Fix:** predict complete confusion patterns and learning transitions in untouched studies, including cases where simpler rules produce worse learning. Compare sequential processing, clustering, rule learning and joint interactions under matched resources. An apparent higher-order choice interaction can arise from mixed strategies; it does not identify primitive cognitive operations.
