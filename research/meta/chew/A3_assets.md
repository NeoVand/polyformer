## 1. The *Nature* Bar and Brutal Audit of Standalone Assets

Recent cognitive-AI papers in *Nature*—such as **Lake & Baroni (2023, *Nature* 623:115–121)**, which showed a standard sequence-to-sequence network trained via meta-learning across compositional tasks matches human systematic generalization, and **Binz et al. (2025, *Nature* / arXiv:2410.20268)**, which fine-tuned Llama-3.1-70B ("Centaur") on 10M+ human choices across 160 experiments to predict held-out human cognition and align with neural activity—succeeded because they used clean experiments to settle foundational scientific questions about intelligence, not because they pitched an engineering benchmark or a synthetic operator.

Judged against that bar, **none of our five assets can reach *Nature* standalone**:

1. **Higher-order, Newton, Feynman, and Free-Field Attention ([FORMALISM.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/FORMALISM.md), [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md)) — CANNOT.**
   - *Definitions:* **Newton attention** computes $k$-subset feature products in $O(nKr)$ time using power sums and **Newton's identities** (classical algebraic recursions expressing elementary symmetric polynomials in terms of power sums, avoiding $O(n^k)$ enumeration). **Free-field attention** combines softmax, query-weighted subset attention regularized by a vacuum parameter $\mu$, and a multi-hop matrix walk propagator $(I-uA)^{-1}$. **Feynman attention (`DS-PA`)** iterates a tree-level recursion of pairwise and cubic interactions across shared rounds.
   - *Why it fails the bar:* On natural language (enwik8 bytes and 400M FineWeb-Edu tokens), subset and Newton attention yield zero perplexity gain over softmax (1.652 vs. 1.650 bits/char; 3.714 vs. 3.684 nats/token in [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L38)) at $1.9\text{--}2.6\times$ the wall-clock cost. Natural text lexicalizes multi-way relations through pairwise syntax and hub words ([WHITEBOARD2.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/WHITEBOARD2.md#L35-L40), Law 2). Feynman attention's 62k vs. 154k pointer-chasing win ([AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L93-L98)) relies on four weight-tied propagation rounds rather than cubic interaction, and fails completely on Match-4 (50.0%) due to the **statistical-query leap barrier** (**Abbe, Boix-Adsera & Misiakiewicz, 2023, COLT / arXiv:2304.03752** — proved SGD learning time is governed by the lowest nonzero Fourier degree, or "leap," of the target function).
2. **Sketchpad / Savant Certification as a Calculator or Table Executor ([paper.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md), [META_REPORT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/META_REPORT.md)) — CANNOT.**
   - *Definitions:* A **pinned-sheet certificate** verifies a model's logit **margin** (the logit gap between the true token and runner-up, bounding numerical drift) on all 2,446 finite local 3-cell views so [`thm-p`](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md#L58-L60) guarantees exactness at all lengths.
   - *Why it fails the bar:* The local-view certification principle is owned by **Moore (1956, *Automata Studies*, pp. 129–153)** (proved no finite experiment identifies an unbounded-state machine), **Cai, Shin & Song (2017, ICLR / arXiv:1704.06611)** (proved bounded recursive views reduce unbounded-length neural execution correctness to finite input checks), **Abbe, Bengio, Lotfi, Sandon & Saremi (2024, NeurIPS / arXiv:2406.06467)** (introduced history eviction and positional resetting in the Inductive Scratchpad), **Rodionov & Prokhorenkova (2025, ICML / arXiv:2402.11628)** (proved out-of-distribution correctness for discrete-state neural algorithmic reasoners, DNAR), and **Lewandowski, Machado & Schuurmans (2026, arXiv:2601.08061)** (proved finite rule verification certifies universal $L(U_{15,2})$ computation in Llama-4 decoding). Worse, in [paper.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md#L38) a Python runtime moves the window and runs Knuth's outer loops, while the dialogue adapter loses 5.8 points on fresh GSM8K ([paper.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md#L133)) and Savant/Loom loses to its base model on user prompts (0.54 vs. 0.73 in [META_REPORT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/META_REPORT.md#L48)).
3. **Standalone Nerode Minimal Automata, Holonomy, and the Characters Law ([THEOREMS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md), [GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md)) — CANNOT.**
   - *Definitions:* **Myhill–Nerode equivalence** groups histories that yield identical outputs for all future continuations into the minimal deterministic finite automaton (DFA); **holonomy attention** assigns each token a non-commuting rotation $U_t \in \mathrm{SO}(d)$ to multiply ordered subsets; the **characters law** states that Match-$k$ balanced accuracy scales as $\frac{1}{2} + F/80$ with the number $F$ of learned **group characters** (homomorphisms from a group to roots of unity that diagonalize group convolution).
   - *Why they fail standalone:* Recovering a 960-state minimal automaton in a ~20D affine subspace ([r8_status.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/rounds/r8_status.md#L43-L45)) is classical **Hankel-matrix spectral realization** (**Hsu, Kakade & Zhang, 2009, COLT / arXiv:0811.4413** — proved SVD of the past-by-future probability matrix recovers minimal linear state spaces), and our robust cell theorem ([`robustMyhillNerode_full`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L514)) was scooped by **Brandoit et al. (2026, arXiv:2609.32447)** (proved an iff invariant-cell contraction theorem for length-independent state tracking). Similarly, learning Fourier characters and **irreducible representations** (minimal invariant matrix subspaces of a group, or irreps) is owned by **Nanda, Chan, Lieberum, Smith & Steinhardt (2023, ICLR / arXiv:2301.05217)** (reverse-engineered modular-addition grokking into discrete Fourier rotations) and **Chughtai, Chan & Nanda (2023, ICML / arXiv:2305.13534)** (showed networks learn group irreps and characters on finite groups), while [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L68-L72) exposed BOS contamination and test-threshold leakage in the characters law.

## 2. The Three Best Recombinations for a *Nature*-Level Result

To reach *Nature*, *Nature Machine Intelligence*, or *Nature Human Behaviour*, we must recombine our verified assets to answer a **scientific question** where we hold a causal mechanism and a theorem. *Assumptions check:* All three options drop Neo's Condition 10 ("train a new foundation architecture from scratch now" in [NEO_CONDITIONS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/NEO_CONDITIONS.md#L41-L45)) in exchange for a 2–4 week experimental path on existing models.

### 1. The Mathematical and Cognitive Law of Working Memory (Sketchpad Theorem Pair + `BRIDGE` + `UNIVERSAL`)

- **Draft Title:** *Working Memory Requires Erasure: How Autoregressive History Corrupts an Intrinsic Symbolic Computer in Language Models and Human Placekeeping*
- **Recombination:** Invert the Sketchpad/Savant story from "we built a calculator" to a fundamental discovery about neural and human working memory:
  1. **Intrinsic computer (`UNIVERSAL`, [INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L176-L200), [INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L305-L320)):** Ordinary text pretraining spontaneously constructs a symbolic table-lookup circuit across all 10 major LLM families (emerging in OLMo-2 between 5B and 84B tokens); a single 2,560-parameter vector or rank-1 edit unlocks 98–99.7% rule execution without external tools (satisfying Conditions 1, 2, 4, 5 in [NEO_CONDITIONS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/NEO_CONDITIONS.md#L17-L32)).
  2. **Why chain-of-thought fails (`BRIDGE` + [`no_finite_test_suite`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L84)):** When history stays in context, unpinned runs collapse at 1,811–8,192 steps ([INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L114), [INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L355)). Crucially, 49,084 of 49,087 steps *after* the first error still execute the transition rule ([INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L120)): 94–100% of first failures are **placekeeping** errors (misreading the event pointer), driven by $\Theta(\log^{L-1} n/n)$ causal attention dilution ([OVERNIGHT_2026-10-28.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/OVERNIGHT_2026-10-28.md#L388)) and pattern-separation collisions (state cosine $>0.3$ increases errors $55\times$, [INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L204)).
  3. **Active erasure ([`policy_tick_eq`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L89)):** Evicting prior steps under a KV-cache attention-sink policy restores bitwise infinite-horizon exactness (80/80 across 10 families vs. 4/80 with history, [OVERNIGHT_2026-10-28.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/OVERNIGHT_2026-10-28.md#L376)).
- **The Missing Experiment:** (i) Map the developmental phase transition of the lookup circuit across dense OLMo-2 and Pythia checkpoints against **Olsson et al. (2022, *Transformer Circuits* / arXiv:2209.11895)** (showed two-layer induction heads drive in-context learning emergence) to prove whether symbolic lookup is a distinct second phase transition; and (ii) put the LLM pointer-vs-step error curve and cosine-interference law directly onto human procedural interruption data from **Altmann, Trafton & Hambrick (2014, *J. Exp. Psychol. Gen.* 143:215–226)** (showed momentary interruptions double human placekeeping sequence errors while leaving step calculation errors unchanged).
- **The Risk:** If the lookup circuit's emergence across OLMo-2 checkpoints is 100% collinear with standard induction heads, the developmental half reduces to Olsson et al. (2022).
- **Live Prior-Art Check:** **Hahn & Rofin (2024, ACL / arXiv:2402.09963)** (proved input-sensitive functions occupy isolated sharp minima in softmax transformers) and **Köver, Butoi, Svete, Hahn & Cotterell (2026, arXiv:2606.08768)** (mapped parameter-space sensitivity geometry for Boolean functions in encoders) leave the **causal decoder fixed-margin dilution bound** and the **10-family placekeeping/erasure law** open.

### 2. Discovering Natural Language's Syntactic Monoid via Two-Sided Nerode Congruence + Holonomy + Causal Steering

- **Draft Title:** *The Algebraic Syntactic Monoid of Natural Language Extracted and Causally Verified Inside Pretrained Language Models*
- **Recombination:** Combine Round 8's O1 ([r8_status.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/rounds/r8_status.md#L9-L17)), our Nerode affine code, `GROUPS.md`'s irrep probe, and Sketchpad's causal steering ([paper.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md#L145-L147)) to execute Zellig Harris's distributional discovery procedure on English:
  1. **Two-sided Myhill quotient:** While Nerode equivalence is one-sided (past $\to$ future), **Myhill's syntactic congruence** relates spans $u \equiv v$ iff $xuy \leftrightarrow xvy$ across all left/right contexts $(x,y)$, forming the **syntactic monoid** (the finite algebraic semigroup of state transitions induced by phrases).
  2. **Spectral group-vs-aperiodic probe ([GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md#L106-L124)):** By **Krohn–Rhodes theory** (**Liu, Ash, Goel, Krishnamurthy & Zhang, 2023, ICLR / arXiv:2210.10749** — proved shallow transformers simulate automata via Krohn–Rhodes parallel prefix shortcuts), every monoid decomposes into **aperiodic** (counter-free) resets and **finite groups**. Apply `rep_content` from [GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md#L121) to the extracted span transition matrices in Qwen3.5-4B to measure directly where natural syntax is star-free/aperiodic vs. group-like.
  3. **Causal verification ([`eq-steer`](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md#L146)):** Use Sketchpad's class-mean activation addition $h_\ell \leftarrow h_\ell + \mu^\ell_{C'} - \mu^\ell_C$ on residual states to prove that swapping spans within a Myhill class preserves downstream distributions while cross-class steering causally flips syntactic admissibility.
- **The Missing Experiment:** Run the 2-week pilot in [chomsky_gate_report.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/chomsky_gate_report.md#L60-L62) using meaning-controlled minimal pairs, prove causal interchangeability via class-mean steering at mid-layers, and take 5 boundary clusters where the model's syntactic monoid splits a category that textbook grammars merge to a pre-registered human acceptability study ($N=100$ subjects).
- **The Risk:** Natural syntax is context-free/mildly context-sensitive; if finite-context clustering fails to reach $\ge 80\%$ held-out oracle fidelity at $\le 200$ rules, the finite quotient is too lossy.
- **Live Prior-Art Check:** **Hu, Wilcox, Song, Mahowald & Levy (2026, *TACL* 14:124–146)** (proved raw string probabilities conflate grammaticality with message plausibility across 280k sentence pairs, validating minimal-pair contrasts); **Bhattamishra, Hahn & Kanade (2026, ICLR, unverified beyond gate)** (extracted DFAs approximating truncated LM support on formal languages); and **Kryvosheieva et al. (2026, ACL, unverified beyond gate)** (causally localized agreement units). None extracts a two-sided syntactic monoid of English, decomposes its group irreps, or confirms a novel human syntactic restriction.

### 3. Solving "World-Model Detour Fragility" via Nerode Entity Quotients + Non-Abelian Holonomy (`r8_status.md` C1 + `GROUPS.md`)

- **Draft Title:** *Resolving the Illusion of State: Quotienting Entities before Group Composition Unlocks Coherent World Models in Sequence Networks*
- **Recombination:** Address two landmark negative results—**Vafa, Chen, Rambachan, Kleinberg & Mullainathan (2024, NeurIPS / arXiv:2406.03689)** (showed via Myhill–Nerode suffix metrics that generative models with high next-token accuracy have incoherent internal world models that collapse under detours) and **Merrill, Petty & Sabharwal (2024, ICML / arXiv:2404.08819)** (proved transformers and state-space models sit in $\mathsf{TC}^0$ and fail non-solvable group state tracking like $A_5/S_5$)—by joining our three October mechanisms:
  1. **Why world models fail on text ([r8_status.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/rounds/r8_status.md#L72-L91), [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L122)):** Standard models fail because (a) repeated entity mentions create spurious $k$-tuples that destroy character orthogonality, (b) commutative attention hits the exact Plancherel order-blind ceiling on non-abelian transitions (77.23% on $A_5$, 83.05% on $A_4$ in [GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md#L18)), and (c) reached-state training is Jacobian-blind ([`blindness`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L178)) and confuses initial with current owners under aliasing ([AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L122)).
