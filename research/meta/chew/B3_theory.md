## 1. Mathematical Audit: What Holds vs. What Breaks

### A. Nerode, Myhill, and Dilution ([THEOREMS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md), [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L112-L128))
- **Definitions:** **Myhill–Nerode equivalence** groups past histories $u, v$ into the same state if every future continuation $w$ yields the same output ($uw \leftrightarrow vw$); it matters because its equivalence classes are the unique minimal state space of any sequential system. **Myhill's syntactic congruence** is the two-sided version ($u \equiv v \iff \forall x,y,\, xuy \leftrightarrow xvy$), whose classes form the **syntactic monoid** (the finite algebraic semigroup of state-transition maps induced by spans).
- **What holds (Lean-verified):**
  - [`blindness`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L178) and [`likelihood_is_blind`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L216): Any objective reading only reached states on training prefixes leaves tangent Jacobians $DF_x(c)$—and thus contraction rates—completely unconstrained. (Partially anticipated on finite sets by **Whitney, 1934, *Trans. AMS* 36:63–89**, who proved smooth functions extend from closed sets with independent derivatives, and **Green, Dietrich,iske & Kevrekidis, 2026, arXiv:2606.12990**, who showed recursive forecasting losses underidentify off-manifold Jacobians.)
  - [`robustMyhillNerode_full`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L514): Block-contraction $\lambda < 1$ around Nerode class balls guarantees infinite-horizon exactness under perturbations $\le (1-\lambda)\min_q r_q$. (Scooped as a standalone theorem by **Brandoit et al., 2026, arXiv:2609.32447**, who proved an iff invariant-cell contraction theorem for length-independent recurrent state tracking.)
- **What broke in our diagnosis:** [CHEW_CONTEXT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/CHEW_CONTEXT.md#L46) and [r8_status.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/rounds/r8_status.md#L80-L85) blame the dilution failure on "averaging over futures instead of quantifying ($\forall$) over them." **That math is wrong** ([AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L121)): on a finite deterministic machine with full symbol support and $\gamma\in(0,1)$, the discounted average $d(u,v) = \sum_{t\ge 0}\gamma^t \mathbb{E}_w[\mathbf{1}\{o(uw_{\le t})\ne o(vw_{\le t})\}]$ vanishes *if and only if* $\forall w,\, o(uw)=o(vw)$. The real bug in `T8.3'` ([AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L122)) is **group-action non-identifiability**: ownership transfers are permutations $\pi_w \in S_4$. Because permutations are bijective, any pair with different *initial* owners $o_0(u)\ne o_0(v)$ and a shared suffix $w$ satisfies $\pi_w(o_0(u)) \ne \pi_w(o_0(v))$ at *every* step. A static code for the initial owner $o_0$ achieves the exact same distance target ($1$ everywhere) as the running state $\pi_w(o_0)$! Identifying the transition requires **collision pairs** ($\pi_u(o_0) = \pi_v(o'_0)$) where distinct prefixes merge.

### B. Higher-Order, Newton, and Holonomy Attention ([FORMALISM.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/FORMALISM.md), [GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md), [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L60-L110))
- **Definitions:** A **group character** $\chi$ maps group elements to complex phases so addition becomes multiplication ($\chi(x+y)=\chi(x)\chi(y)$); an **irreducible representation (irrep)** is a minimal matrix analogue for non-abelian (order-dependent) groups. They matter because testing $x_1+\dots+x_k=0$ reduces to summing products of characters via **Newton's identities** ($m e_m = \sum_{j=1}^m (-1)^{j-1}e_{m-j}P_j$).
- **What holds:**
  - **Order-$k$ mean blindness ([AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L106)):** For uniform independent $X_1,\dots,X_k \in G$, any function of $<k$ variables has identical class means on $\sum_{i=1}^k X_i = 0$ vs. $\ne 0$.
  - **Character witness identity & Parseval SNR law ([FORMALISM.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/FORMALISM.md#L250-L263)):** Over $\mathbb{Z}_3^4$, character orthogonality gives exact witness counts at width $(|G|-1)/2=40$ for all arities $k$. With $F \le 40$ conjugate character pairs pooled over $M=\binom{n-1}{k-1}$ subsets, non-zero sums have mean $-F/80$ and variance $81F(40-F)/6400$, yielding balanced accuracy $\mathrm{BA} \approx \Phi\!\left(\alpha\sqrt{F/(40-F)}\right)$ with $\alpha \propto M^{-1/2}$.
  - **Non-abelian $\mathrm{SO}(d)$ obstruction ([GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md#L347-L361)):** Commutative ($d=2$) layers are capped at the order-blind Bayes ceiling ($83.05\%$ on $A_4$, $77.23\%$ on $A_5$). Because $\exp(A)\in\mathrm{SO}(d)$ has $\det=+1$, one holonomy layer represents $A_5$ faithfully at $d=3$ ($\det=1$ icosahedral irreps), whereas $S_5$'s 4D irreps map transpositions to reflections ($\det=-1 \in \mathrm{O}(4)\setminus\mathrm{SO}(4)$), requiring $d=5$ ($4\oplus\mathrm{sign}\subset\mathrm{SO}(5)$) or $n_h=4$ Householder reflections (**Siems et al., 2025, ICLR / arXiv:2502.10297**, who proved $n_h=n-1$ Householder steps track $S_n$).
- **What broke ([AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L17), [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L68-L72), [AUDIT.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/survive/AUDIT.md#L107-L109)):** (i) Including `BOS` in `polybench.py` contaminates arity $m$ via $e_m(\text{cards}\cup\{\text{BOS}\}) = e_m(\text{cards}) + \phi_{\text{BOS}}e_{m-1}(\text{cards})$; (ii) dividing by $\binom{t}{m}$ before RMS-normalization with floor $10^{-6}$ crushes arity-4 features by $45\times$ at $t=256$; (iii) without-replacement Match-4 pair marginals are slightly non-uniform (39 vs. 38 completions), and $F=40$ is necessary only for *pooled* subset sums, not single tuples (where 4 basis characters suffice).

### 1. Settling "Impossible Languages" via Syntactic Monoid Aperiodicity ([A4_wildcard.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/A4_wildcard.md#L36) + [A3_assets.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/A3_assets.md#L34) + [GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md))
- **Why [A4_wildcard.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/A4_wildcard.md#L40) needs our math:** Matching possible vs. impossible languages only on minimal-DFA state count $|Q|$ is mathematically insufficient—$A_5$ and $\mathbb{Z}_{60}$ have identical $|Q|=60$ ([GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md#L90)), and a counter-free reset DFA can match a modulo-$p$ counter DFA in both $|Q|$ and description length!
- **The Theorem Spine:** By **Schützenberger (1965, *Inf. Control* 8:190–194)** (proved a regular language is **star-free** / first-order definable iff its syntactic monoid is **aperiodic**, having no nontrivial subgroups, $x^n=x^{n+1}$) and **Krohn & Rhodes (1965, *Trans. AMS* 116:450–464)** (proved every finite automaton decomposes into aperiodic resets and simple groups), the algebraic dividing line is **aperiodicity vs. group counters**. In mathematical linguistics, **Graf (2022, *Theor. Linguist.* 48:145–184)** showed human syntactic dependencies on movement tiers are subregular and **aperiodic**, while **Yang, Chiang & Angluin (2024, NeurIPS / arXiv:2310.13897)** proved masked transformers without position arithmetic express exactly star-free (aperiodic) languages, and **Merrill, Petty & Sabharwal (2024, ICML / arXiv:2404.08819)** proved $\mathsf{TC}^0$ transformers fail non-solvable group tracking.
- **The Spine in Action:** Construct posibles/impossibles matched *mechanically in Lean* on $|Q|$, alphabet, and dependency distance (eliminating the locality confound found by **Hunter, 2025, *Comput. Linguist.* 51:2** in **Kallini et al., 2024, ACL / arXiv:2401.06416**), differing *only* in whether the syntactic monoid is aperiodic ($x^n=x^{n+1}$) vs. group-periodic ($x^p=e$). Use our two-sided Myhill probe, `rep_content` irrep detector ([GROUPS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/GROUPS.md#L121)), and causal class-mean steering ([`eq-steer`](file:///Users/neo/repos/sketchpad/paper/paper.md#L146)) inside Qwen3.5-4B (using meaning-matched minimal pairs per **Hu, Wilcox, Song, Mahowald & Levy, 2026, *TACL* 14:124–146**) alongside human learning.

### 2. The Working-Memory Trilemma in Machines and Human Placekeeping ([A3_assets.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/A3_assets.md#L23) + [A2_language.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/A2_language.md#L43))
- **The Theorem Spine:** Our Lean triad—[`no_finite_test_suite`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L1498), [`window_loses_place`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L1644), and [`policy_tick_eq`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L1690)—proves a formal impossibility trilemma for working memory:
  1. Retaining history corrupts execution via unsnapped context drift ([`no_finite_test_suite`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L1498));
  2. Bounding the window without an external pointer loses **placekeeping** (tracking position in a procedure) on low-entropy stretches once $2m \ge k+2|S|$ ([`window_loses_place`](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/THEOREMS.md#L1644));
  3. Active history eviction (`policy_tick_eq`) restores exact execution across all 10 LLM families (80/80 vs. 4/80 with history, [OVERNIGHT_2026-10-28.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/OVERNIGHT_2026-10-28.md#L376)).
- **The Spine in Action:** This theorem directly explains why 94–100% of LLM chain-of-thought collapses in `BRIDGE` are placekeeping errors while 49,084/49,087 subsequent steps still execute the rule ([INSIGHTS.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/INSIGHTS.md#L114-L120)), and unifies it quantitatively with human procedural interruption laws (**Altmann, Trafton & Hambrick, 2014, *J. Exp. Psychol. Gen.* 143:215–226**; **2017, *J. Exp. Psychol. Appl.* 23:216–229**, who showed interruptions selectively elevate human placekeeping errors while sparing step calculation).

### 3. The Fourier-Leap Law of Relational Working Memory ([A1_nature_anatomy.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/meta/chew/A1_nature_anatomy.md#L50) + [FORMALISM.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/FORMALISM.md))
