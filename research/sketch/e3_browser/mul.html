Canvas multiplier: long multiplication on the model's own sketchpad

Each tick the model reads a 3-cell strip of the canvas through its own eyes and writes two glyphs and the name of its next step. The cursor's position is the loop counter; the canvas is the whole memory. Click a digit of the multiplier that the model has not reached yet to change it.
