- **C1 (`NARROW`) & C4 (`NARROW / OPEN`) — The Open Wedge**:
  - **Mark Shapiro** ([arXiv:2608.11233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11233), **31 Jul 2026**) retrofitted a 6.01M rank-16 LoRA loop into `Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct` with exact per-step intermediate-state supervision on pointer chasing, and **Tracr-Injection — Vergara-Browne & Soto** ([arXiv:2505.10719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10719), **15 May 2025**) distilled compiled `tracr` programs into `GPT-2-large` via a linear projection + IIT orthogonal-complement roll + FineWeb KL before discarding the compiled model.
  - **Why both fail OOD**: Neither includes a discrete attractor snap or margin certificate. Shapiro's retrofitted Qwen loop collapses from 97.7% at $d=12$ to **10.9% at $d=22$** ($1.5\times$ depth), and Tracr-Injection is feed-forward only (0.9% on 4-digit addition).
  - **What remains genuinely open (`C1 + C4` on `C5`)**: Distilling a **looped** ALTA/SUBLEQ-style universal register/stack interpreter ([arXiv:2410.18077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18077), **23 Oct 2024**, explicitly left open in Appendix C.1) into a browser-sized looped LM (`ByteDance/Ouro-1.4B` or LoRA-looped `Qwen3-0.6B`) with **codebook snapping + full-domain transition margin + FineWeb KL**, discarding the compiled teacher, and certifying the trained loop operator by **finite enumeration** so it extrapolates to $10{,}000+$ exact latent steps without token CoT.
