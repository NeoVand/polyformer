**C1 — The conjunction survives; the components do not.**

STARS already combines recurrent-dynamics analysis, Jacobian spectral-radius regularization and randomized loop counts. Its synthetic addition model reaches reported 100% accuracy; its pretrained Ouro experiment improves depth robustness. Neither establishes an exact universal executor or exhaustive transition certificate. [STARS, 26 May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26733v1).

Shapiro already installs a reusable, stepwise latent procedure in pretrained Qwen while testing capability retention. Thus “learned computation installed inside an intact LM” is insufficient differentiation. [Shapiro, 31 July 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11233).

The certificate strategy also has close antecedents: finite local checking supports universal computation through extended language-model decoding. **Auditor’s mathematical objections, 29 October 2026:**

- Contractivity does **not** imply exact history independence. It can shrink historical differences without eliminating them.
- For code states \(q\), prove regions \(B_q\) satisfy \(F_a(B_q)\subseteq B_{\delta(q,a)}\), with correct decoding throughout. Checking centres alone is insufficient unless execution actually resets to them.
- A globally finite machine supports unbounded run duration, not unrestricted universal storage. Specify extensible memory, finite local access, addressing and initialization.
- “Every length” means correct finite execution prefixes; it cannot mean every program halts.
- Certify the **merged, deployed numerical implementation**, including quantization, routing, caches and halting. KL training is not a retention certificate.
- “No host clock” should mean no host-supplied algorithmic schedule. Some runtime must still iterate the block.

Related theory explicitly distinguishes semantics-preserving correction within predictive equivalence classes from destructive contraction between distinguishable states. [*What Can a Recurrent State Safely Forget?*, 20 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23366).

**C2 — Generated weights are old; certified executable weights remain distinguishable.**

Text-to-LoRA generates adapters from task descriptions; Drag-and-Drop maps prompt batches to LoRA matrices. Sakana’s later demonstration turns documents into adapters. These kill “language generates useful weights,” but do not establish program-counter semantics or exact instruction execution. [T2L, 6 June 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06105); [DnD, 19 June 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16406); [Sakana, February 2026](https://pub.sakana.ai/doc-to-lora/).

**Deduction:** certificates for individual deltas do not certify their sums, orderings or interactions with base weights. Prove composition, instruction selection and shared-state invariants. Otherwise C2 is adapter generation with a programming metaphor.

**F — “The register is the state” has a direct predecessor.**

Qwenputer explicitly stores **code, data, registers and program counter in Gated DeltaNet state**. One-hot keys overwrite individual rows; one-hot queries read them; α and β are fixed to 1 for RAM. Its RISC-V implementation adds eleven compiled layers before Qwen’s layers. This kills novelty in “a computer’s addressable registers live inside Qwen3.5’s delta state.” [Announcement, 22 Oct 2026](https://divagr.com/blog/qwenputer); [repository, inspected 29 Oct](https://github.com/divagr18/qwenputer).

The distinction matters: these weights are compiled, not learned through projection-only LoRA; execution produces special trace tokens. The RISC-V driver loads firmware into state and excludes chip tokens from Qwen’s layers. The accelerated path executes the circuit’s intermediate representation. **It does not establish F’s full conjunction.** [Implementation notes, inspected 29 Oct 2026](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/divagr18/qwenputer/main/experimental/riscv/docs/rv32.md).

Schlag already presents delta updates as elementary fast-weight programming instructions. Independent erase/write control also has explicit successors: Gated DeltaNet-2 and Erase-then-Delta Attention. Renaming addresses “registers” supplies no mechanism novelty. [Schlag, 22 Feb 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11174); [GDN-2, 21 May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22791); [EDA, 25 Jun 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26560).

A particularly relevant new experiment fine-tunes hybrids on answer-only parity. Qwen3.5-4B reaches mean **0.55 at 128 flips**, versus **0.89 for OLMo-Hybrid**, after training through 64; capping OLMo’s β at 1 destroys extrapolation. This is a small, author-run experiment, but directly challenges “stock Qwen’s recurrence makes learning exact tracking straightforward.” [Agrawal, 22 Oct 2026; raw results](https://github.com/divagr18/hidden-reasoning).

The requested theoretical neighbors establish different things: negative-eigenvalue expressivity, Householder-product expressivity, REPL-trained variable tracking, and an optimization intervention. None supplies F’s certificate for a pretrained Qwen checkpoint. [Grazzi, 19 Nov 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12537); [DeltaProduct, 14 Feb 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10297); [Siems, 16 Feb 2026, revised 25 Jun](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14814); [Howe, 16 Jul 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18966).

**The locality calculation is elementary; closing it indefinitely is the contribution.** For fixed update coefficients, unit register keys and \(G_{ij}=k_i^\top k_j\),

\[
(S'-S)k_j=(\alpha-1)Sk_j+
\beta(v-\alpha Sk_i)G_{ij}.
\]

Orthogonality eliminates write interference, **not global decay**. Small Gram entries provide one-step bounds, not arbitrary-duration correctness. You need bounded write residuals, numerical-error bounds and invariant basins for the complete transition—including control and other memory channels.

**Reject the architectural “iff” as stated.** Fixed-dimensional real state is not automatically finite; recurrent state is history-dependent. What helps is a sound finite abstraction of all behaviorally relevant state. Finite inductive proofs are not generally restricted to finite-state systems. A transformer that ignores its historical cache can be certifiable without a host. A Qwen hybrid still has attention caches, convolution state and positional machinery; pinning/windowing is an additional execution policy. Merrill’s result concerns specified SSM expressivity, not this proposed certifiability classification. [Merrill et al., ICML, 21–27 Jul 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/merrill24a.html).

Also, Kimi’s channel-wise KDA and RWKV-7’s generalized delta dynamics require separate proofs; they are not interchangeable instances of the displayed scalar-gated equation. [Kimi Linear, 30 Oct 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692); [RWKV-7, 18 Mar 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14456).

**Q1 — What survives S1–S5?**

**S1:** Brandoit’s result concerns particular finite-precision state-tracking architectures and perturbation assumptions. It does not establish that rasterization makes a VLM exact. A hard glyph decision followed by canonical rendering can remove within-symbol analog variation; it can also preserve a confidently wrong symbol forever.

The necessary certificate covers **reader → transition → symbol selection → renderer**, plus control state. Checking isolated glyphs cannot certify a globally attentive vision encoder whose outputs depend on neighboring content, resizing and positions. Quantizing a latent codebook can provide the same discontinuous reset without pixels.

**S2:** Locality and shared weights make an inductive proof possible; they do not supply one. Exhaustive enumeration works when the alphabet and neighborhood are finite, the deployed update truly depends only on that neighborhood, and every allowed pattern implements the intended rule. Reachability restrictions require their own proof. Carry propagation, boundaries and halting also matter.

“Constant size” must mean constant **during one computation**. Arbitrarily long inputs require a family of larger canvases. Also, retained text, KV caches or recurrent hidden states invalidate a claim that the canvas alone is the Markov state.

**S3:** Fewer sequence positions is not automatically greater reliable information capacity, fewer bytes or less computation. Vision tokens are continuous vectors, and recovering plausible prose tolerates errors that arithmetic cannot. The useful experiment measures tokens/cell against worst-case symbol margin and whole-computation failure, with fixed typography, quantization and perturbation bounds.

**S4:** Integer storage does not itself conserve anything. Each collision/update must preserve specified invariants; streaming and boundaries must preserve their totals; overflow must be excluded. A rendered ball position omits velocity, so it is not generally a complete Markov state. One next-canvas loss is a shared training format, not evidence that arithmetic and physics share a learned algorithm.

**S5:** Make the intervention consequential: replace a carry, bead or velocity while clearing every other persistent state; require the continuation predicted from the edited state. Blank/shuffled-image controls are necessary. [Wang et al., 6 Aug 2026, revised 8 Sep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06270) found visual tool outputs often lacked causal influence despite apparent reasoning gains. Handwriting recognition remains a separate fallible interface: exact arithmetic conditional on transcription is not exact interpretation of arbitrary photographs.

**Q2 — Optical compression: actual numbers and actual memory**

[DeepSeek-OCR, 21 Oct 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18234v1) reports approximately **97% OCR precision below 10× text/vision-token compression**, falling toward **60% at 20×**. Its English Fox experiment contains 100 pages with 600–1,300 text tokens. Examples:

| Text tokens/page | Vision tokens | Compression | OCR precision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 600–700 | 100 | 6.7× | 98.5% |
| 900–1,000 | 100 | 9.7× | 96.8% |
| 1,200–1,300 | 64 | 19.7× | 59.1% |

These are not exact-page success rates. Fixed modes use **64/100/256/400 tokens** at **512/640/1024/1280 square pixels**; cropping changes totals. Its OmniDocBench comparison uses under 800 tokens against MinerU’s 6,000-plus.

“Optical forgetting” proposes rendering history and progressively reducing resolution/token allocation. The paper explicitly leaves validation beyond OCR to future work; it does not demonstrate exact recurrent working memory.

**Q1 — What survives S1–S5?**

**S1:** Brandoit’s result concerns particular finite-precision state-tracking architectures and perturbation assumptions. It does not establish that rasterization makes a VLM exact. A hard glyph decision followed by canonical rendering can remove within-symbol analog variation; it can also preserve a confidently wrong symbol forever.

The necessary certificate covers **reader → transition → symbol selection → renderer**, plus control state. Checking isolated glyphs cannot certify a globally attentive vision encoder whose outputs depend on neighboring content, resizing and positions. Quantizing a latent codebook can provide the same discontinuous reset without pixels.

**S2:** Locality and shared weights make an inductive proof possible; they do not supply one. Exhaustive enumeration works when the alphabet and neighborhood are finite, the deployed update truly depends only on that neighborhood, and every allowed pattern implements the intended rule. Reachability restrictions require their own proof. Carry propagation, boundaries and halting also matter.

“Constant size” must mean constant **during one computation**. Arbitrarily long inputs require a family of larger canvases. Also, retained text, KV caches or recurrent hidden states invalidate a claim that the canvas alone is the Markov state.

**S3:** Fewer sequence positions is not automatically greater reliable information capacity, fewer bytes or less computation. Vision tokens are continuous vectors, and recovering plausible prose tolerates errors that arithmetic cannot. The useful experiment measures tokens/cell against worst-case symbol margin and whole-computation failure, with fixed typography, quantization and perturbation bounds.

**S4:** Integer storage does not itself conserve anything. Each collision/update must preserve specified invariants; streaming and boundaries must preserve their totals; overflow must be excluded. A rendered ball position omits velocity, so it is not generally a complete Markov state. One next-canvas loss is a shared training format, not evidence that arithmetic and physics share a learned algorithm.

**S5:** Make the intervention consequential: replace a carry, bead or velocity while clearing every other persistent state; require the continuation predicted from the edited state. Blank/shuffled-image controls are necessary. [Wang et al., 6 Aug 2026, revised 8 Sep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06270) found visual tool outputs often lacked causal influence despite apparent reasoning gains. Handwriting recognition remains a separate fallible interface: exact arithmetic conditional on transcription is not exact interpretation of arbitrary photographs.
