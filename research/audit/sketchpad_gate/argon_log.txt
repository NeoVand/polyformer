### Summary of Verdicts in [argon_out.md](file:///Users/neo/repos/polyformer/research/audit/sketchpad_gate/argon_out.md)

* **`S1` (Rendering is restoration) — `NARROW`**:
  * **Killed components**: The exact mathematical core (R1 decodable code + R2 restoration, NFSM argmax/round restoration $\Phi_x(h)=\mathrm{rd}(\theta_x\mathrm{rd}(h))$, and finite transition-table enumeration) is proven in [Brandoit et al., arXiv:2609.32447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32447) (**26 Sep 2026**). Step-wise rounding over local bit templates for exact arbitrary-length binary arithmetic and Turing-complete `SBN` is proven in [Back de Luca et al., arXiv:2502.16763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16763) (**24 Feb 2025**, v4 **30 Jan 2026**). The memoryless Markovian visual canvas overwrite loop ("Inductive CoS", $C_t \to C_{t+1}$) is already implemented in [Lotfi et al., *Chain-of-Sketch*, arXiv:2410.08165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08165) (**10 Oct 2024**, v2 **26 Jun 2025**).
  * **Still open**: Realizing the non-affine restoring map $\mathrm{rd}(\cdot)$ specifically through **discrete 2D glyph rendering + VLM optical re-reading** (or snapped 2D VQ glyph grids) as an external restoring organ for a pretrained VLM with certified margin $\ge 2\eta$.
* **`S2` (Locality gives size generalisation) — `NARROW`**:
  * **Blunt architectural flaw**: Pretrained VLMs are **not** $3\times 3$-local or shift-equivariant (global ViT attention layers, 2D-RoPE or interpolated absolute positional embeddings, causal decoder ordering). Enumerating $5^9 = 1{,}953{,}125$ $3\times 3$ neighborhoods does **not** certify an $H\times W$ VLM update unless the VLM read-write operator is architecturally restricted to a sliding $3\times 3$ crop or local shared-weight head.
* **`S3` (Optical compression as denser working memory) — `NARROW`**:
  * **Killed high-compression claim**: [Liang et al., *Visual Merit or Linguistic Crutch?*, arXiv:2601.03714](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03714) (**7 Jan 2026**) proves that [DeepSeek-OCR, arXiv:2510.18234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18234) (**21 Oct 2025**) relies on the LLM's language prior at ~7–10× compression; on non-linguistic/random symbols (shuffled words, random characters, or UUIDs in [Glyph, arXiv:2510.17800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17800), **20 Oct 2025**), accuracy collapses from ~90% to **~20%**. Multi-resolution "optical forgetting" is also explicitly proposed in [DeepSeek-OCR Sec. 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.18234) and implemented in [OCR-Memory, arXiv:2604.26622](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26622) (**29 Apr 2026**).
  * **Still open**: Measuring the **zero-prior minimum-logit-margin curve** (vision tokens per glyph cell vs. worst-case adversarial digit grids) where $100\%$ exact restoration holds without language priors.
* **`S4` (The physics link) — `NARROW`**:
  * **Killed CA conservation claim**: Exact local mass conservation in both continuous NCAs (`MaCENCA`) and discrete integer CAs (`MaCE Discrete CA` via integer division + central-cell remainder, yielding solitons in ~83% of random samples) is published in [Papadopoulos & Guichard, *MaCE*, arXiv:2507.12306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12306) (**16 Jul 2025**) and [Momeni et al., arXiv:2606.23115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23115) (**22 Jun 2026**).
  * **Still open**: Training **one pretrained VLM** under a single next-canvas objective to execute both certified multi-digit arithmetic and exact integer conservative lattice dynamics on the same discrete 2D glyph canvas.
* **`S5` (The demos) — `NARROW`**:
  * **Still open**: While interactive pokable NCA canvases ([Growing NCA](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/), [MaCE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12306)) and VLM sketching/handwritten-math pipelines ([SketchVLM, arXiv:2604.22875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22875); [arXiv:2605.19043](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19043)) exist, no client-side small-VLM demo executing a live, pokable, Markov-overwritten mental-abacus or long-multiplication grid to certified completion (or webcam handwritten sum $\to$ live Markov glyph canvas) was located as of 29 Oct 2026. ([`onnx-community/Qwen3.5-0.8B-ONNX`](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Qwen3.5-0.8B-ONNX) at $256\times 256\text{ px} = 64\text{ tokens/frame}$ is the fastest browser-ready candidate.)
