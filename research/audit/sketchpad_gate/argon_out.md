## Q1. Direct Prior Art on the Visuospatial Sketchpad Thesis (`S1`–`S5`)

* **`S1` (Rendering as Restoration & Markov Canvas Overwrite)**:
  * **[Brandoit et al. (arXiv:2609.32447, 26 Sep 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32447)** occupies the exact mathematical core: Theorem 3.1 proves length-independent finite-state tracking at precision $\eta>0$ is equivalent to **(R1) Decodable code** ($\pi\circ\iota=\mathrm{id}_Q$, separation $\ge 2\eta$) and **(R2) Restoration** ($\Phi_x(U_q)\oplus\bar{B}_\eta\subseteq U_{\delta_x(q)}$). Theorem 4.1 proves affine recurrences realize at most *definite* automata at any $\eta>0$. Section 6 defines the Neural Finite-State Machine $\Phi_x(h)=\mathrm{rd}(\theta_x\mathrm{rd}(h))$ via winner-take-all rounding $\mathrm{rd}(h)=\mathbf{1}_{\arg\max_j\mathbf{1}_j^\top h}$, and Appendix H.6 certifies all lengths by finite enumeration of reachable states and inputs (App. C.1 traces restoring organs to von Neumann 1956, Omlin & Giles 1996, Casey 1996, Maass & Orponen 1998).
  * **[Back de Luca et al. (arXiv:2502.16763, 24 Feb 2025; v4 30 Jan 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16763)** proves in the NTK limit that hard rounding at every step over local bit templates prevents noise accumulation across arbitrary-length binary addition, multiplication, and Turing-complete `SBN`.
  * **[Lotfi et al. (arXiv:2410.08165, 10 Oct 2024; v2 26 Jun 2025, *Chain-of-Sketch*)](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08165)** Sec. 3.3 ("Inductive CoS") already implements the Markov frame-overwrite loop ($C_t\to C_{t+1}$ + halt bit) and notes drift in continuous MSE pixel predictions.
  * **Still open**: Realizing $\mathrm{rd}(\cdot)$ via **discrete 2D glyph rendering + VLM optical re-reading** as an external restoring organ for a pretrained VLM.
* **`S2` (Locality & $5^9$ Neighborhood Enumeration)**:
  * **Blunt objection**: Pretrained VLMs are **neither $3\times 3$-local nor shift-equivariant** (global ViT attention, 2D-RoPE/interpolated pos embeddings, causal decoder ordering). Checking $5^9=1{,}953{,}125$ $3\times 3$ patches does **not** certify an $H\times W$ VLM update unless the VLM is applied as a cropped $3\times 3$ sliding window (or local shared-weight head), or cross-patch attention leakage outside $3\times 3$ is bounded strictly below $\eta$.
* **`S3` (Optical Compression vs. Exact State)**:
  * **[Liang et al. (arXiv:2601.03714, 7 Jan 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03714)** refutes high-compression exact symbol recovery: 7–10× optical compression relies on the LLM's language prior; on random words/characters (and UUIDs in [Glyph, arXiv:2510.17800](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.17800)), accuracy drops from ~90% to **~20%**. Exact state tracking requires low compression ($\ge 1$ vision token per $1$–$4$ cells).
* **`S4` & `S5` (Conservative Physics & Browser Demos)**:
  * Exact mass conservation in continuous and integer CAs is solved by [Papadopoulos & Guichard (*MaCE*, arXiv:2507.12306, 16 Jul 2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.12306v1). Open: unifying certified arithmetic and conservative lattice-gas rules in one VLM next-canvas model (`S4`) and shipping the browser small-VLM mental-abacus ([Frank & Barner 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21767040/)) and handwritten-sum Markov sketchpad demos (`S5`).

## Q4. Exact Arithmetic & Conservative World Models

1. **Exact Multi-Digit Arithmetic**:
   * **1D Text Transformers with Structural Positional Alignment**: **[Abacus Embeddings (arXiv:2405.17399, 27 May 2024; v2 23 Dec 2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.17399v2)** reaches **99.1%** on 100-digit addition ($5\times$ OOD) and **99.9%** on 15-digit multiplication; **[Position Coupling (arXiv:2405.20671, 31 May 2024; v2 30 Oct 2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.20671v2)** & **[Cho et al. (arXiv:2410.15787, 21 Oct 2024; v2 17 Apr 2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.15787v2)** reach **>98.5%** on 200-digit addition ($6.67\times$ OOD) and generalize across length and count; **[Tiny Transformers (arXiv:2610.09003, 6 Oct 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09003)** reaches **>99.99%** on 10-digit addition ($<1\text{M}$ params).
   * **2D Grid Automata & Step-Wise Rounding**: **[Neural GPU (arXiv:1511.08228, 25 Nov 2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.08228)** & **[Improved Neural GPU (arXiv:1702.08727, 28 Feb 2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.08727)** generalize from 20-bit to **2,000-bit** binary addition/multiplication on a 2D CGRU grid; **[Back de Luca et al. (arXiv:2502.16763, 24 Feb 2025; v4 30 Jan 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16763)** proves exact arbitrary-length binary addition, multiplication, and `SBN` execution via local template matching + step-wise rounding.
   * **VLMs on Visual/Handwritten Math**: **[CogAlign (arXiv:2502.11492, 17 Feb 2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.11492v3)**, **[Skripkin et al. (arXiv:2511.11704, 12 Nov 2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11704v1)**, **[FERMAT (arXiv:2501.07244, 13 Jan 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07244)**, and **[Handwritten Math Grading (arXiv:2605.19043, 18 May 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19043)** (87% of errors are handwriting transcription mistakes) do not use a Markov 2D arithmetic sketchpad.
2. **Exact Conservation in CAs vs. Video World Models**:
   * **Conservative CAs & PDEs (`S4` prior art)**: **[Papadopoulos & Guichard (*MaCE*, arXiv:2507.12306, 16 Jul 2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.12306v1)** enforces exact local mass conservation $m_i^{t+1}=\sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}m_j^t\frac{\exp(\beta A_{ji})}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal{N}(j)}\exp(\beta A_{jk})}$ in continuous **`MaCENCA`** and integer **`MaCE Discrete CA`** ($\beta\to\infty$ with integer division + central remainder), finding **solitons in ~83% of random samples**. **[Momeni et al. (arXiv:2606.23115, 22 Jun 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23115)** studies mass-conserving NCA reservoirs. Continuous PDE conservation uses divergence-free forms (**[arXiv:2210.01741, 4 Oct 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01741)**) or projection layers (**[Liu et al., arXiv:2505.24579, 30 May 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.24579v2)**; **[PhysicsCorrect, arXiv:2507.02227, 3 Jul 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02227v2)**, $100\times$ error reduction).
   * **Video World Models**: **[Veo 3 (arXiv:2509.20328, 24 Sep 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20328)**, **[Genie 3 (Aug 2025)](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/)**, **[V-JEPA 2 (arXiv:2506.09985, 11 Jun 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985)** / **[V-JEPA 2.1 (arXiv:2603.14482, 15 Mar 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14482)**, and **[Cosmos (arXiv:2501.03575, 7 Jan 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03575)** / **[Cosmos 3 (arXiv:2606.02800, 1 Jun 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02800)** have **zero exact conservation laws**.
