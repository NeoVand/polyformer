## The story (from research/CONTRIBUTIONS.md, sections A-G)
1. Next-token training is blind to small per-step state errors (Theorem: blindness), so models fit the training length
   and drift beyond it.
2. Exactness at length needs three ingredients: a reachable code (coverage), closure (a fixed norm), and a state-level
   signal that is stiff in the per-step error. Certified equivalences (pairs of histories a checker says end in the
   same state) are the strongest such signal; with closure the joint minimiser is the minimal (Nerode) automaton.
3. One table across seven settings (research/master/master_table.md on the notes branch, generated by build_master.py;
   never type a number by hand): only the full recipe with closure is exact on every seed everywhere; every partial
   recipe fails somewhere.
4. One object explains the table: the error cocycle (THEORY_DEEP). In-law exactness follows its Lyapunov exponent;
   every-input exactness its graph-constrained joint spectral radius. Each ingredient acts on a different part.
5. Robustness: closure-trained trackers with the signal survive every repeated-cycle attack (0 of 3,840 on 6 of 6
   seeds); fixed norm alone breaks (32-363); snapped trackers are certified exact on every input at every length.
6. Mechanism: the exact state is the minimal automaton (960 clusters), with error correction inside finite basins.
7. Toward language models: coverage plus self-distillation takes a 0.5B model's whole-history chain of thought from
   0 to 0.88-0.92 at 256 statements with no label beyond 16 (oracle 0.94-1.00; the registered bar was missed
   narrowly); the teacher's error is 17 times higher on states short training never reaches.
8. Limits, stated once and plainly: "exact" means on typical inputs unless snapped; addition needs only closure; Lean
   has a shallow floor at 0.445; S5 sits near the threshold; language without a perfect checker is stable but not exact
   (coverage, not wrong pairs); physics: closure must be onto the invariant set and phase cannot be repaired; frontier
   models solve the stories and half of long additions with tens of thousands of reasoning tokens.
